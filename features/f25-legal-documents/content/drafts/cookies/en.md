## 2. What this policy covers

This policy explains how cookies, browser local storage, and similar technologies are used on postqron.com and within the Postqron application. Your choices about non-essential technologies are collected through a consent mechanism that organizes technologies into four categories: **necessary**, **preferences**, **analytics**, and **marketing**. Only the **necessary** category is active before you make any choice. The other three categories require your explicit opt-in, and nothing classified under them loads or runs until you have given consent.

## 4. No analytics, preferences, or marketing trackers today

As of this draft, Postqron does not set or load any cookie, pixel, SDK, or script under the **preferences**, **analytics**, or **marketing** categories. These categories exist within the consent mechanism for potential future use, are strictly opt-in, and currently have zero registered trackers. Nothing is tracked, measured, or targeted under them today. If this changes, the inventory in Section 3 will be updated and users will be asked for a fresh consent choice before any new non-essential technology is loaded.

## 5. Your choices

On your first visit, no optional technology loads before you make a choice. The consent banner presents "Accept all", "Reject all", and "Customize" with equal visual prominence, and no options are pre-ticked in your favor. A recorded choice is stored for a maximum of 6 months. You may review or change your choice at any time using the "Review your cookie choices" link available in the site footer or your account area; revoking consent takes no more steps than granting it. Withdrawing consent immediately stops any new reads or writes of non-necessary technologies, and any cookies you can access under those categories are removed.
