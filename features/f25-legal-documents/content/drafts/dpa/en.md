# Postqron Data Processing Agreement

*Draft version 0.1 — pending legal review. This document describes, in good faith and for internal and preview purposes, the data processing terms that apply between Postqron and its business customers. It is not yet a finalized legal instrument.*

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is issued by APDSoftware, operator of the Postqron service, reachable at help@postqron.com and via https://apdsoftware.it. The registered contracting entity's full legal name, registered office, and VAT number are recorded as pending legal-review metadata and will be disclosed here once confirmed.

## 1. Parties and roles

This DPA applies between Postqron, acting as processor, and a business customer using the Postqron service, acting as controller, with respect to personal data contained in content that the customer schedules or publishes through Postqron on the customer's documented instructions ("Customer Content Data").

For account-level data — including the customer's own end users, workspace membership, and billing metadata that Postqron collects and uses to operate, secure, and bill for the service — Postqron acts as an independent controller in its own right, under the terms of its Privacy Policy. This DPA does not override, limit, or otherwise affect that independent controllership. This DPA governs exclusively the processing that Postqron carries out on the customer's instructions with respect to Customer Content Data.

## 2. Subject matter, duration, nature, and purpose of processing

2.1 **Subject matter.** The subject matter of this DPA is the provision by Postqron of social-media scheduling and publishing services to the customer, as further described in the Terms of Service.

2.2 **Duration.** This DPA has the same duration as the underlying Terms of Service between the parties and terminates automatically when that agreement ends, without prejudice to the return and deletion obligations set out in Section 13 below.

2.3 **Nature of processing.** Processing carried out by Postqron under this DPA includes the storage of Customer Content Data, its scheduling, its transmission to the social media platforms selected by the customer, and its display within the Postqron product interface.

2.4 **Purpose of processing.** Processing is carried out solely for the purpose of providing the contracted service to the customer, and for no other purpose.

## 3. Categories of data and categories of data subjects

3.1 **Categories of personal data.** The categories of personal data processed are determined by the customer and consist of whatever personal data the customer chooses to include in the content it uploads, composes, or schedules through the service. This may include, at the customer's discretion, names, images, contact details, or other personal data embedded in that content, together with associated metadata describing scheduling and publishing outcomes (such as timestamps, target platform, and delivery status).

3.2 **Categories of data subjects.** The categories of data subjects are likewise determined by the customer's own content and normally include the customer's audience, employees, or contacts referenced or depicted in that content.

3.3 Postqron has no visibility into, and exercises no control over, the specific categories of personal data or data subjects that a customer chooses to include in its content; the customer remains solely responsible for ensuring that such inclusion, and the underlying processing it instructs, has a valid legal basis.

## 4. Documented instructions

4.1 Postqron processes Customer Content Data only on the documented instructions of the customer, including instructions given through the customer's ordinary use of the product's features (composing, scheduling, and publishing content) and through this DPA and the Terms of Service, unless otherwise required by applicable law, in which case Postqron will inform the customer of that legal requirement before processing, unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

4.2 If, in Postqron's assessment, an instruction from the customer infringes the GDPR or another applicable data protection provision, Postqron will inform the customer without undue delay.

## 5. Confidentiality

Postqron ensures that personnel authorized to process Customer Content Data are bound by an appropriate obligation of confidentiality, whether arising from contract or from statute, and that access to Customer Content Data is limited to personnel who need it to perform their role in providing the service.

## 6. Security measures

Taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of processing, as well as the risks to data subjects, Postqron implements appropriate technical and organizational measures, consistent with Article 32 GDPR and with the security measures described in Postqron's Privacy Policy, including in general terms:

- encryption of data in transit using TLS;
- encryption at rest for sensitive fields, such as OAuth tokens, which are stored separately from the data they protect;
- role-based access control over internal systems and data;
- structured audit logging of sensitive actions performed within the service;
- regular encrypted backups, periodically tested for restore integrity.

Further exploitable architectural detail is deliberately not disclosed in this document, consistent with sound security practice.

## 7. Sub-processors

7.1 The customer grants Postqron a general authorization to engage sub-processors for the purpose of providing the service.

7.2 Before adding or replacing a sub-processor that will process Customer Content Data, Postqron gives the customer's workspace Owner at least thirty (30) days' prior notice, keeps a record of that notice, and provides a channel through which the customer may raise a reasoned objection before the new sub-processor is activated on the customer's workspace. If the customer raises a timely and reasoned objection, activation of the new sub-processor on that customer's workspace is suspended until the objection is resolved in accordance with this Section 7.

7.3 The current, authoritative, and regularly updated list of sub-processors — including each sub-processor's role, the service it provides, the categories of data it processes, its location, and the applicable international transfer mechanism where relevant — is maintained and published as the separate "Subprocessors" legal document and registry, which is incorporated into this DPA by reference and should be consulted for up-to-date detail rather than relying on any list reproduced elsewhere.

## 8. International transfers

Postqron selects its own infrastructure and sub-processors with an EU/EEA-first hosting preference. Where any transfer of Customer Content Data outside the European Economic Area is necessary, Postqron relies on an applicable adequacy decision of the European Commission or on appropriate safeguards, such as the Standard Contractual Clauses, as verified and disclosed on a per-vendor basis in the Subprocessors registry referenced in Section 7.3 above, which constitutes the authoritative and up-to-date source describing the exact transfer mechanism relied upon for each sub-processor.

## 9. Assistance with data subject rights

Taking into account the nature of the processing, Postqron provides the customer with reasonable technical and organizational assistance for the fulfilment of the customer's obligation to respond to requests from data subjects exercising their rights under Chapter III GDPR (including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection) with respect to Customer Content Data. This assistance is provided primarily through in-product tools that allow the customer to manage and delete its own content, and, where needed, through direct support available at help@postqron.com.

## 10. Assistance with security obligations and data protection impact assessments

Postqron assists the customer, as reasonably required and taking into account the information available to Postqron and the nature of the processing, with the customer's own obligations under Articles 32 to 36 GDPR, including the carrying out of data protection impact assessments and, where applicable to the customer's use of the service, prior consultation with a competent supervisory authority.

## 11. Personal data breach notification

Postqron notifies the customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Content Data. The initial notification includes the information reasonably available to Postqron at that time, and Postqron provides reasonable further updates as its assessment of the breach progresses, consistent with Articles 33(2) and 28(3)(f) GDPR.

## 12. Audit rights

Postqron makes available to the customer the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and with Article 28 GDPR, and allows for, and contributes to, audits, including inspections, conducted by the customer or by an auditor mandated by the customer, subject to reasonable advance notice, confidentiality obligations, and scheduling arrangements that do not disproportionately disrupt Postqron's operations or the service provided to its other customers. Consistent with Article 28(3)(h) GDPR and calibrated to the realities of a multi-tenant SaaS operator, the default form of audit is a documentation-based compliance review; on-site or inspection-based audits remain available within these reasonable bounds where documentation review does not adequately address the customer's audit needs.

## 13. Return and deletion of Customer Content Data

13.1 At the end of the service relationship between the parties, and at the customer's choice, Postqron returns or deletes Customer Content Data, except to the extent that applicable law requires Postqron to retain some or all of that data.

13.2 Deletion follows the same operational mechanics already defined for account and workspace closure under the service: a confirmed deletion request opens a twenty-eight (28) day grace period during which the relevant data is frozen and recoverable on request; after that period, the data is deleted or anonymized; encrypted backups containing that data become unrecoverable on their normal expiry cycle of at most thirty-five (35) days; and a minimal tombstone record is retained only to the extent necessary to reapply the deletion in the event that a disaster-recovery restore reintroduces previously deleted data.

## 14. Liability and term

14.1 Liability arising under this DPA is governed by, and subject to the same limitations and exclusions as, the liability section of the Terms of Service between the parties, which applies to this DPA as if set out in full.

14.2 This DPA remains in force for as long as Postqron processes Customer Content Data under the underlying agreement between the parties, and terminates automatically, without further action by either party, upon termination of that agreement, subject to Section 13 above.
