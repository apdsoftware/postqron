# Postqron Privacy Policy

*Draft version 0.1 — pending legal review. This document describes, in good faith and for internal and preview purposes, how Postqron processes personal data. It is not yet a finalized legal instrument.*

## 1. Controller and contact

This notice is issued by Apdsoftware di Carlo Zuffetti (Via C. Colombo 15, 24047 Treviglio (BG), Italy, VAT number 03835250162), trading as APDSoftware, the controller for the Postqron service (entity information verified via a public source: https://mailronix.com/terms, consulted 2026-07-25), reachable at help@postqron.com and via https://apdsoftware.it. No Data Protection Officer has been appointed. For all privacy-related questions, requests, or complaints, please use help@postqron.com.

## 2. Categories of personal data processed

Postqron processes the following categories of personal data in connection with the service:

- **Account and profile data**: name, email address, and authentication credentials or OAuth identifiers used to sign in.
- **Workspace data**: workspace membership, roles, and permissions assigned within a workspace.
- **Content data**: drafts, uploaded media, scheduling metadata, publishing destinations, and publishing outcomes that the user creates or uploads through the service.
- **OAuth identity data**: where a user signs in through Google, Apple, Facebook, or LinkedIn, Postqron receives the email address, display name, avatar image, and provider account identifier supplied by that provider, used solely to create and authenticate the account.
- **Billing metadata**: plan tier, seat or usage quantities, and workspace-level billing status visible to Postqron. Full payment instrument data (card numbers, bank details, and similar) is held exclusively by Paddle, acting as Merchant of Record, and is not held by Postqron.
- **Usage and security audit logs**: records of actions taken within the service, generated for security, accountability, and troubleshooting purposes.
- **Cookies and similar technologies**: necessary, preferences, analytics, and marketing categories may be used. The full, current inventory of cookies is maintained in the separate Postqron Cookie Policy and is not duplicated here. As of this draft, only "necessary" cookies are in active use.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Providing the account, workspace, scheduling, and publishing service | Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of a contract |
| Billing, invoicing, and responding to lawful requests from competent authorities | Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR — legal obligation |
| Security, abuse prevention, and the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims | Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest, documented internally through a legitimate-interest assessment |
| Publishing content to the social platforms the customer selects | Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for account-level data; contractual instructions and data processing terms where Postqron acts as processor for content data processed on the customer's documented instructions, depending on the exact data flow and the receiving platform's own terms |
| Strictly transactional and security emails (e.g. login alerts, password resets, billing notices) | Art. 6(1)(b), (c), or (f) GDPR depending on the trigger; sent from infrastructure kept separate from marketing communications |
| Optional marketing communications from Postqron | Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR — consent, together with the special regime applicable to electronic marketing communications; opt-in only, never pre-ticked, with an unsubscribe mechanism in every message |
| Non-essential cookies and trackers | Consent, under the applicable ePrivacy framework; as noted above, no non-essential cookies are currently active |
| Handling export, deletion, objection, and other privacy-rights requests | Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR — legal obligation; typically actioned through an authenticated in-account workflow |

## 4. Recipients

Postqron shares personal data, to the extent necessary for the purposes above, with the following categories of recipients:

- **Mailronix** — transactional email delivery.
- **Paddle** — payment processing, acting as Merchant of Record.
- **Hetzner** — cloud hosting infrastructure.
- **Cloudflare** — DNS, content delivery, edge network, and TLS termination services.
- **OAuth identity providers** (Google, Apple, Facebook, LinkedIn) — each acts as an independent controller for the login and identity-verification step; that step is governed by the relevant provider's own privacy notice, and these providers are not Postqron subprocessors.
- **Social publishing platforms** selected by the customer (for example, Facebook Pages or Instagram Professional accounts) — these are the customer's own connected accounts and integrations, used to schedule and publish content at the customer's instruction.

A public, regularly updated subprocessor registry (the Postqron "Subprocessors" document) provides the full, current, per-vendor detail of parties processing personal data on Postqron's behalf and is the authoritative source for that information; it is referenced from this notice rather than duplicated here.

## 5. International transfers

Postqron and its infrastructure providers are selected with a preference for EU/EEA-based hosting. Where personal data is nonetheless transferred outside the European Economic Area, that transfer relies on an applicable adequacy decision or on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, verified on a per-vendor basis. The subprocessor registry referenced in Section 4 is the authoritative, up-to-date source for the specific transfer mechanism applicable to each vendor; this Privacy Policy does not duplicate that per-vendor detail.

## 6. Retention

Personal data is retained according to the following schedule:

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account, profile, and workspace-membership data | For the life of the account |
| Inactive accounts | After 24 months of inactivity, an inactivity-closure process begins, with advance notice at 30 and 7 days before closure |
| Confirmed deletion requests | A 28-day grace period follows, during which data is frozen and usable only to cancel the request, meet a legal obligation, or handle a security incident, before deletion or anonymization |
| Deleted content | Recoverable for 7 days, then permanently deleted within 24 hours |
| Media never attached to any content | Deleted after 24 hours |
| Media detached from all content | Deleted after 7 days |
| OAuth tokens | Revoked and locally deleted within approximately 15 minutes of disconnection, revocation, or the start of a deletion process |
| Sessions | Invalidated within approximately 5 minutes |
| Audit logs | 12 months |
| Operational logs | 30 days, extendable to up to 90 days where tied to an open security incident |
| Encrypted backups | A maximum of 35 days |
| Data export packages | 7 days, with download links expiring after 24 hours |

## 7. Security

Postqron applies the following technical and organizational measures, among others: encryption in transit (TLS) for all traffic to and from the service; encryption at rest for sensitive fields such as OAuth tokens, held separately from the data they protect; role-based access control governing internal access to systems and data; structured audit logging of sensitive actions; and regular encrypted backups with periodic restore testing.

## 8. Data subject rights

Subject to the conditions set out in the GDPR, data subjects have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict the processing of, port, and object to the processing of their personal data (Arts. 15–21 GDPR). Most of these rights can be exercised directly through an authenticated in-account export or deletion workflow; others may be exercised by contacting help@postqron.com.

Data subjects also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Italy, the competent authority is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (https://www.garanteprivacy.it). Under Art. 77 GDPR, a data subject may also lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of their own EU member state of habitual residence or place of work, if different from Italy.

## 9. Minors

The Service is restricted to users aged 18 or over and is not offered to individuals under the age of 18, aligned with the minimum-age requirement adopted by Buffer, the comparable service used as a commercial benchmark (source: Buffer, Terms of Service, Section 2 "Eligibility" — "You must be at least 18-years old to use the Service", https://buffer.com/legal/terms-of-use/year/2023, consulted 2026-07-25).

## 10. Complaints and DPO governance

Privacy-related requests and complaints should be directed to help@postqron.com. No Data Protection Officer has been appointed.
