# Cookie Policy

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies Postqron uses. It complements
the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

## 1. The short version

Postqron uses **very few cookies, and almost all of them are strictly necessary**. We
do not use advertising cookies, we do not sell data to anyone, and nothing that is not
strictly necessary runs before you agree to it.

## 2. What we use

### 2.1 Strictly necessary

These make the service work. They do not require consent, and you cannot turn them off
without breaking sign-in.

| Purpose | Kind | Retained |
|---|---|---|
| Keeping you signed in | Cookie, `HttpOnly`, `Secure`, `SameSite` | Session duration |
| Protecting forms against cross-site request forgery | Cookie | Session duration |
| Remembering the language you chose | Cookie or local storage | 12 months |
| Remembering your cookie choices | Cookie | 6 months, after which we ask again |

The language cookie deserves a word: it stores a preference you expressed, nothing
about who you are, and without it the site would forget your language on every visit.

### 2.2 Everything else

At the time of writing this version we use **no** analytics, advertising, profiling or
social media cookies.

Analytics are planned. When they arrive, this policy gets a new version listing the
purpose, the provider, the retention and the legal basis — consent — and the cookies
stay blocked until you give it. They will not appear under "strictly necessary",
because measuring how a site is used is not necessary to make it work.

### 2.3 Cookies set by others

- **Paddle** sets cookies during checkout, necessary to complete payment and to
  prevent fraud. They are governed by Paddle's own cookie policy.
- **Cloudflare** may set a strictly necessary cookie to protect the site from abusive
  traffic.

We do not embed third-party content that tracks you across sites.

## 3. Your choices

When you first visit, a banner asks what you accept. **Refusing is exactly as easy as
accepting**: both are a single click, at the same level, with the same prominence. We
do not use a design that nudges you toward accepting.

Non-essential cookies and technologies are **blocked until you agree to them**, not
set first and cleaned up afterwards.

You can change your choice at any time from the link in the footer, and you can also
delete cookies in your browser — though doing so will sign you out and reset your
language.

## 4. How long consent lasts

We record what you chose, when, on which version of this policy and in which language.
We ask again when this policy changes materially, or after
6 months.

## 5. Changes

We may update this policy. The version and effective date are at the top; material
changes reset consent.
